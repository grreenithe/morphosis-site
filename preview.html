FAQ

Questions?

Do I need to be experienced?

No. The coaching is designed to work with your current experience level. Your program will start where you are.

Do I need a gym?

No. Your training can be designed around the equipment you actually have access to.

How much time do I need to train?

Your program will be designed around your schedule. The goal is to find the minimum effective structure you can consistently execute.

What if I don't know how to eat?

That's part of what you're being coached on. You'll receive personalised calorie and nutrition guidance and learn how to make adjustments over time.

How does support work?

You'll have direct text support during designated coaching hours, alongside your weekly check-ins.

How long should I stay coached?

That depends on your goals and progress. Coaching is designed to teach you how to make better decisions rather than keep you dependent on a coach forever.
